Checking connection…

On the Teaching Hub, open an assignment’s IC scores and click “Send to the Chrome helper”. In Infinite Campus, open that assignment’s score panel and click “Hub helper”. It shows every student and what it will do; nothing is typed until you tick its box and click Enter. Your school login stays in your browser.

It keeps the higher score (fills blanks and raises lower scores, never lowers one), leaves IC flags alone, then saves, reloads and checks every score.

Keep both tabs open until the result is confirmed. An uncertain save requires checking Infinite Campus before retrying.

Install the same generated package in Chrome or Edge on each computer you use. School browser policy may require your administrator to allow it.
