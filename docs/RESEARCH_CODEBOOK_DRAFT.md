# Research observation guide from Answerable Teaching

## Purpose and review status

The primary question is **Are students getting better at conceptualizing science
concepts?** The secondary questions concern engaged, epistemic and authorial
agency in their written work. This draft translates the supplied manuscript
into an open observation guide for teacher review. It is not a validated scale,
an automatic classifier, a grade rubric or a ranking of students.

Source: *Answerable Teaching — Full Revised Decision Draft*. Relevant passages
were read directly from the supplied Word file and key pages visually checked.
The manuscript was not edited. Its complete content and student cases are not
copied into this repository. The old separate research document has not been

## What each category means

| Dimension | Source-grounded meaning | Important boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conceptual understanding | Discern and reason with critical aspects and relationships; use the concept to explain, compare and consider changed cases. | This is a synthesis of the book's account, not a single quoted definition or an existing numbered rubric. |
| Engaged agency | Bring one's own thinking and purposes to understanding the assigned disciplinary object within the lesson frame. | Partial or mistaken thinking can show agency; correctness, completion or polished writing alone cannot establish it. |
| Epistemic agency | Make a bid to change the question, the task's frame, or what counts as knowledge or a solution. | A thoughtful answer or an alternative conclusion within the same frame is not automatically an epistemic bid. |
| Authorial agency | Author one's own purposes and path of study rather than select among purposes supplied by the teacher. | Own-word answers are not sufficient. Distinguish an emerging purpose from documented follow-through on a self-authored path. |

## Proposed indicators of conceptual development

These five observation prompts are **my operational proposals grounded in your
book**, not five stages attributed to you. Each lesson still needs its actual
concept, critical aspect and disciplinary criteria identified.

1. **Discernment:** Does the response distinguish the relevant feature from an
   incidental or surface feature? P575-P582, P605.
2. **Relationships:** Does it explain how or why parts, conditions or processes
   relate, rather than merely name them? P574-P575, P585-P586.
3. **Use across cases:** Can the learner apply a distinction to a contrast or
   new case and recognize a limit to an earlier generalization? P580-P585, P593.
4. **Reasoned consequences:** Can the learner predict what a change would do and
   support the prediction with reasons, including qualifications when visible?
   P585-P586.
5. **Refinement:** Across comparable work, what conception changed, and what
   evidence supports that interpretation? Record the student's explanation of
   the change when available, without fabricating one. P640-P647.

Record both the reasoning visible and its disciplinary fit. A response may
reveal a meaningful new relationship while still containing a scientific error;
neither erase that development nor affirm the error as correct. A short answer
may demonstrate more conceptual control than a longer one.

P602 mentions four stages but does not enumerate them in the passage that
follows. This guide does not invent that missing four-stage scheme. The older
research document may contain a fuller operationalization; compare it if supplied.

## How to read agency evidence

Start with the actual move and the assigned task, then consider a category.
Record where the move appears to originate, its object, the decision made,
the reasons or response to evidence, and the classroom opportunity for it.
These follow Chapter 2 P161-P170, not a trait test.

An engaged-agency candidate might explain a self-devised approach to an assigned
comparison. An epistemic-agency candidate would instead question the comparison's
framing or what ought to count as an explanation. These are illustrative
descriptions, **not quotations or findings from students**. In Forensics, doing
the assigned revision step of challenging one's conclusion does not by itself
show that the student reframed the knowledge task. Keep prompted response and
student-originated reframing distinguishable.

An authorial-agency candidate needs evidence that the learner has created a
purpose and direction of inquiry, not merely written an interesting optional
question. A proposal may merit attention before it has become a sustained path.
Keep evidence of the bid, the teacher's reception and follow-through separate:
an unrecognized bid is not necessarily an absent bid (Chapter 1 P90).

Two categories may describe different moves in one episode. An unfamiliar move
may remain descriptively unclassified. Do not force all writing into a category
or count categories as ascending achievement levels. Chapter 11 P1032 calls
for an open case index rather than a closed rubric; Chapter 12 P1071 treats the
categories as changing learner-task-context configurations, not kinds of people.
Lurking, playing/messing around and geeking out can inform context, but cannot
be reliably assigned from a blank box or a writing sample's length alone
(Chapter 2 P231; P598-P600).

## Evidence and growth records

Preserve exact student words, source revision and slide/response location,
prompt, date evidence, relevant support, interpretation, alternatives,
uncertainty and teacher review. Student writing belongs only in approved private
records, not this codebook or public HTML.

For each concept, the eventual view should put **earlier evidence → later
evidence → what changed → what remains uncertain** together. Within-lesson
revision and development across lessons must remain separate. This implements
the intention of *Growth made visible to the student*, P645 (render pp131-132),
without assuming every pair demonstrates improvement.

Unknown writing order means the direction of change is unverified. Missing
earlier work means insufficient evidence of growth, not no growth. An answer
added later to a first-answer box cannot establish a pre-instruction baseline.
Record mixed or unchanged evidence honestly. Class summaries must show eligible
and missing/noncomparable cases; counts of completed slides are not growth rates.

## Decisions for teacher review

**Authorial scope:** Approved. Record emerging student-designed inquiries
separately from sustained independent learning paths. An emerging inquiry has a
learner-originated purpose and proposed direction; a sustained path has documented
decisions and follow-through over time. Use Chapter 2 P274-P275 and Chapter 8
P850-P851 as anchors. Preserve evidence and earlier records; neither form is a
score, fixed student label or automatic progression.

**Conceptual indicators:** Accept or revise the five open observation prompts
above. They do not add points, performance levels or an automatic cutoff.

**Intellectual authorship:** Chapter 8 P855 and Chapter 12 P1045 distinguish the
broader capacity to make one's thinking consequential from authorial agency as
its rare outermost form. Do not label every own-word conceptualization authorial.
Democratic agency is also in the book, but is not added as a fourth research
target without your direction; retain relevant unexpected observations openly.

## Boundaries and continuation

Teacher review precedes student coding. No student dataset was coded, no findings
were generated, and no completion grades or grade history changed. No research
collection, publication, email or live dashboard update is enabled here.

The draft's discussion of AI-like writing at P633 is not evidence that style
can establish authorship. Do not infer copying or penalize students from polish,
hedging or vocabulary. Preserve known provenance and uncertainty. The book's
general description of two-tier grading also does not replace the separately
approved completion-credit prompts.

The next book revision needs a new fingerprint and a comparison of these cited
passages. Preserve this draft and prior coding versions; flag affected findings
for re-review. This version record is not an active file watcher or monthly sweep.
